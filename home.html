Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about NextHire

What exactly does NextHire do?

NextHire helps you analyze your resume using AI and track all your job applications in one place. You can see how strong your resume is, identify missing skills, and manage applied jobs, interviews, and follow-ups from a single dashboard.

How accurate is the AI resume analysis?

Our AI analysis is based on real job descriptions, ATS rules, and industry standards. While no AI can guarantee a job, NextHire gives highly accurate suggestions for improving keywords, formatting, and content to increase your chances of shortlisting.

Is my resume data safe?

Yes. Your resume and personal data are encrypted and stored securely. We do not sell, share, or use your data for training without your permission. You can delete your resume permanently at any time from your account.

What is ATS and why does it matter?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software used by companies to filter resumes. If your resume is not ATS-friendly, it may never reach a recruiter. NextHire checks your resume structure, keywords, and formatting to improve ATS compatibility.

Can I track multiple job applications?

Yes. You can track unlimited job applications depending on your plan. Each job can have its own status (Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected), notes, reminders, and resume version.

Is NextHire free to use?

Yes. The free plan allows resume analysis and basic job tracking. Premium plans unlock advanced AI suggestions, unlimited tracking, resume versions, and priority support.

Can I use NextHire for different career fields?

Absolutely. NextHire works for software developers, designers, data analysts, business professionals, students, and fresh graduates. The AI adapts feedback based on your selected role.

Does NextHire apply to jobs on my behalf?

No. NextHire does not auto-apply to jobs. Instead, it helps you stay organized, improve your resume, and track where you have applied so you never miss follow-ups.
